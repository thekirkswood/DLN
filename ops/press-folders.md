# Standing asset folders

Campus doctrine. When we **open a house**, pre-folder stills the same way every time. Harvest onto the hub press kit follows those names. The site build and `/epk/{kit}` then share a tree. Journalist packs follow the HT4 map as a **chassis** (cover lanes **Logos / Product / Founder / Campaigns** — DAA: Logos / Fields / People / Office). Each `/epk/{kit}` is that house’s unique pack — unique copy and unique vault. DAA copy is the slim pack `Site/src/data/daa-epk.json` cut from `/home/main/DAA/_meta/assets/daa.json` (`python3 Site/src/data/build-daa-epk.py`). Do not dump brief screenshots into another house. Do not send every View EPK link to one shared module.

ModYu is the backbone (`/home/main/ModYu/Site/public/assets`). Copy the **folder names**, not the look.

## Folders to create on a new house

Under that house’s public root (usually `Site/public/assets/`):

| Folder | What goes in |
|---|---|
| `brand` | Mark, lock-ups, wordmark. Do not invent a mark. |
| `people` | Portraits and team stills they have given. |
| `products` | Pack shots, lineup. |
| `press` | Stills that belong in the journalist pack. |

Keep extra rooms when the product implies them (ModYu: `guide`, `journey`, `lifestyle`, `phases`, `social`, `voices`, `epk`). Do not create empty theatre.

DAA uses `Site/public/brand`, `Site/public/media`, and `assets/logo-tests` — do not force DAA onto ModYu cream folders. Harvest still maps those roots into `press/daa/{brand,media,mark}`.

Skip: `Screenshots`, `archive`, `motion`, `spin`, `play` (DAA play frames stay in the library, not the pack). DAA pack stills: shipped mark, colour fields including gap, Mark Barlow / Greisy Flores / Council, plates, office-01–04. Ban field-gap no longer. Ban `mark-barlow-live` (workshop still).

## Hub copy

`Site/src/lib/press-harvest.ts` copies house folders into gitignored `Site/public/press/{kit}/…`. Assets desk flags pack. `/epk/{kit}` is that pack. Studio edit the copy on the kit itself. The lab launches View site / View EPK; it does not lecture harvest in compose.

If memory always pre-folders, harvest stays sorted. Do not invent a second library.

- Invent a Dave Kirkwood Studio mark
- Harvest brief PNGs as if they were the house
