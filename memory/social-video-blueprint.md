## Shared offer (every door)

Everyone is shown the same object, said to **you**:

- A **live** presence and a constant **sandbox** beside it — one package.
- You leave a **note**. We take it into a **human-led** build.
- **You** choose when it is **pushed** to live.

The **lead** changes with the door. That is the whole strategy. Mix the leads and each market hears the wrong job.

## Avenue A — Entrepreneurs (piece 2)

**Locked bed:** Design Lab North are a space for the idea. Not only a site — a room an investor can sit in. A sandbox while you talk, a live presence when you say go. Tried, human-made, years in the work. You leave the note. We build. You choose when it is pushed.

### Who, from the research (UK 2024–26)

Not GEM’s mass of “everyday entrepreneurs” (5.7m private firms; 75% have no employees). This door is people assembling a **room for several people to walk**.

- **First-time equity:** 2,730 UK companies raised a first round in 2025; median first round down to £200k (H1 2026 £170k). More first-time **deals** outside London than in it. Manchester the largest non-London count. They need a presence *before* the meeting.
- **SEIS / angels:** rounds built from many small tickets; “nobody invests on just a PowerPoint slide.” Solo UK founders are unusually common in VC-backed firms. Fear: looking like one person with a Notion page.
- **Age:** investor-attracting viewer mostly late 20s–early 40s. Not a teen film. Not a grey-temple corporate film.
- **Press-kit-shaped space is rare:** of 334 already-funded UK startups, only 13.2% had a press/news page. That is the opening — not “you need a website.”

Amounts stay in the research file. Not on the film. Do not gender the voice. Do not say where the house lives. North is allowed in the *weight* of the work, not as a costume.

Full groups, never-say, and sources: `memory/entrepreneur-film.md`.

## Avenue B — Small business, new (piece 3)

**Locked bed:** We start with your brand. What we make is yours, not a template. You have a live site and a sandbox beside it. Leave a note. We take it into the build. You say when it goes live.

### Who, from the research

Someone trading, or about to, **without a proper public face**: Facebook, Checkatrade, a parked domain, a mate’s template, or nothing. 68% of UK firms have a website; sole traders 65% — the gap is real. BPE 2025: most private firms are sole / micro; construction is the largest SME sector by count. Who pays is the owner or couple (family-owned is the UK default among SME employers). Spine buyer: **Facebook / Instagram, 35–54**, North West overlay then national. Do not punch London. Do not put a town in the ad.

This is not the entrepreneur door. A consultant on LinkedIn can see a 20s cut; the trade overlay is Facebook.

Full seats and copy tests: `memory/social-avenue-small-business-new.md`.

## Avenue C — Small business, refresh / rebrand / rebuild (piece 4)

**Locked bed:** We look first. Market, conflict, competitor. Then we sit with what you already have live. The sandbox takes the next pass. You choose when the live site moves. We do not talk down what is up now.

### Who, from the research

Someone already findable, slightly ashamed **or** simply tired, who will not buy from a studio that talks down the wall they paid for. Small firms (10–49) already have websites at 93%. Family-owned and family-managed is the UK employer default (~73% / 72%). 82% of family-business leaders see themselves as **stewards**. Shame and adequacy live together: UK work has found nearly half of businesses embarrassed by their site *and* most saying it still meets expectations. Dunking the live site dunks stewardship — and the cousin, intern, or last studio may still be in the room.

Triggers: the street moved; competitors look newer; the firm grew; leads down despite reputation; a handover. Competition is the safe conflict. Point at the **street**, not a red ring on their homepage.

Age older than piece 2. Facebook is the older owner’s main app; TikTok is 17% of leaders over 55 — not this door’s spine. A marketing manager needs a film they can **forward** without insulting the MD.

Full respect rules and UK case shapes: `memory/social-refresh.md`.

## Format lock (all films)

- **15s master** that can live everywhere; **20s** where the platform and the door want air (Facebook feed, YouTube, LinkedIn hybrid).
- **9:16 first** (Reels, Stories, Shorts, LinkedIn vertical). Sandbox **top**. **16:9** second. Sandbox **left**.
- **Mute-first.** Captions after picture lock. Voice after that. Either large-word track **or** VO plus small burnt subs — not a paragraph in the windows.
- Mark **late** or at the end. Never a 0s logo bumper.
- Aktiv Grotesk. Paper / Ink. 45° chamfer. No pills, shadows, orbs, keyboard clacks, music swell, trending audio.

End action: understand the object, then leave a note / come to `designlabnorth.com`. Do not invent `/entrepreneurs`. Do not print Book a call unless Ewan marks a CTA. Do not print amounts.
