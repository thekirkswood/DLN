# Comms — Narrative and Sensory

Own: `BoardComms.tsx`, `/board/comms/{message,channel,audience,spotlight}`. Lasswell is the spine: who says what, in which channel, to whom, with what effect.

## Job

Foundational identity system project preparation. Bedrock of human interaction. On the table this is **Narrative** (the words) and **Sensory** (how it is felt before anyone reads). Not a content calendar.

They populate this. The line has to match print, site, and a conversation.

## Four rooms (must not share a layout)

**Message.** The one line, set like mission: large, short. What it must never say (the second voice you are cutting). Which door currently says something else. Reads mission, promise, proposition, big-idea. If those bits exist, they sit under the line as “what the book already says.” Generate later can propose a line from the book — socket only.

**Channel.** Sensory. Not a platform logo. Three **places the line has to survive**: screen, print, a room. A fourth: where it already breaks. If it only works in a deck, the room should feel unfinished. Stills belong here more than anywhere except personality: a phone, a pack, a hall. Socket until Assets.

**Audience.** Who the line is spoken to, and who it is not. What should happen in them (an effect you could notice, not “engagement”). **Not** the eight Mapping seats. Those are people the plot has to hold in the landscape. This room is the spoken-to of Lasswell. Door: “Eight customer seats” and People. If a seat is named, you may point at it; you may also name a spoken-to who is not a seat yet.

**Spotlight.** The moments that cannot invent a second intent: launch, sitting, pack, host going live. List the next three. The line they share (must be able to equal Message). What is allowed to be quiet. Who holds the script (a person from People, or “not named”).

## Interconnect

Message without Channel is a line that has nowhere to live — show the three places as empty sockets. Spotlight without Message is theatre. Audience without seats can still be held, but Mapping should remain a door. Personality should appear under Message as how the line behaves.

## Multi-format

Channel and Spotlight want stills. Message wants type (Aktiv, not a downloaded font). Generate: “say this line in this channel from this brand” — socket.

## Save

Distinct pack kinds already: `message`, `channel`, `comms-audience`, `spotlight`. Keep them. Deepen fields and layout, do not flatten back to a sheet.

## Look

Message = typographic room. Channel = three chamfered places side by side (screen / print / room). Audience = two columns (for / not) plus effect. Spotlight = a short rundown of moments, like a call sheet, not a blog.

## Done when

You can write one line, name three places it must live, name who it is not for, and list two spotlight moments that share the line. Walking to personality and mission shows the same intent. Channel does not look like Message.

## Do not

A social calendar. Collapsing Audience with Mapping customers. Fake generated ads.
