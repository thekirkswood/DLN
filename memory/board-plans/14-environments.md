## What a room is

The well is an **instrument**, not an article with fields. First paint is the job of that cell. You write on the object. Neighbours are plates still on the table, or a spine of the same faculty. Teaching sits behind (`What this cell holds`). Generate stays a socket.

Guardianship is the current bar: cadence plates, ticks, type large enough to be the UI. Other rooms copy that *depth of instrument*, not the same layout.
