## Each stage as a protocol room

Not eight copies of a Hold box. A **walk**: you are in a stage; you see what must be true to leave it; you see what the plot already holds that this stage reads; you sign or you do not.

**1 Object / The Project.** What the work is. What it is not. What we will hold as true before anyone draws. Reads mission, purpose. Sign: a named owner (pad 1).

**2 People / The People.** Who is in the room. Who has to live the plan. How we talk so the work stays one thing. Reads people bit. Sign: the people named, or “not yet.”

**3 RAD.** Evidence before a line. Door to mapping/evidence and landscape. What you already run, who it is for, what the market needs. Cannot complete honestly if evidence is empty — show the hole.

**4 Concepts / Initial Ideas.** Wide pass. Nothing is the answer yet. Range, then we cut. Reads big-idea as a candidate among others, not the winner yet. Door to workbench ideas.

**5 Candidate / The Concept.** One direction you can hold. Naming, positioning, first system. Reads proposition, positioning. If positioning is a pose (no landscape), this stage says so.

**6 System / The Build.** Made, not described. Print, screen, or the sitting itself. Door to workbench tools. What file is being made.

**7 Implementation.** Leaves the desk. Live site, pack, notes from the session. Door to host. Sandbox vs live named.

**8 Guardianship.** Looked after. We keep coming in. Private preview can reopen. Live stays up. Reads ethics/standards. Sign: who looks, how often.
