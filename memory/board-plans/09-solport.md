# Solport Sessions

Own: `/board/solport/{sitting,tools-in-use,channels,content}`. Name is **Solport** (S-O-L-P-O-R-T). Never Soulport. He is a designer only where DKS says so; on this campus Solport is the sitting faculty.

## Job

One-to-one, online, on the actual work. Leave with the next thing to publish. What is said here feeds back onto the board. Not private notes that vanish.

## Four rooms

**Sitting.** Who is in the sitting. What this sitting is for. What they must leave with today. Reads people.

**Tools in use.** The tools they already have in their hands — not a recommended stack. Door to workbench tools.

**Channels.** Where the brand has to live **this week**. Door to comms channel and location.

**Content.** What goes out, and what it does when it does. Must match Message. Door to landscape token it serves.

## Interconnect

A sitting that does not write a cell is theatre. Content without a landscape cell is a post with no neighbourhood. Pad does not live here.

## Multi-format
