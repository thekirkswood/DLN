# A.P.E.S. — where to look

Own: `BoardApes.tsx`, `BoardApesTopic.tsx`, `apes-play.ts`, compass on the table, Cultural/Social engine socket. **Not** Swarm hive.

## Job

A thinking tool you switch on when the moment matters. It does not tell you what to think. It tells you where to look. Four mindsets are **lenses, not people**. Never label a person as an Analytical thinker.

On Binder3, A/P/E/S sit **on the identity column** — they look at what they populate, they do not replace it.

Campus already has a honed playfield: mindsets, task kinds, cycle, interrogation, cards, TaskEx loop. Keep that depth. What is missing: 15 combinations as rooms or a plate; social engine on Cultural/Social; identity/persona/mask as a usable room; speed-thinking as a short mode.

## Rooms

**Analytical / Practical / Emotional / Social.** Already unique enough to keep as play. Each must show: the ask, how it fails, questions, and **this plot’s** relevant bits (mission, people, personality, ethics) as what that lens is looking at — not a blank exercise.

**Creative / Critical / Decision / 360.** Task kinds. All still walk all four. Creative generates range; critical for and against; decision what-if; 360 hold all four. Record why on decision.

**Cycle.** Launch → Action → Turnaround → Reflection → Landing. Reflection consolidates; it does not reopen. Bounded tasks.

**Cards.** Six campus cards: What is it, Where/who, Why, What could go wrong, What could we do, So what. Keep questions honest until context is understood.

**Interrogation.** Analyse / Probe / Evaluate / Synthesize — how an LLM should walk context later (`10-generate.md`). Human sovereign.

**15 combinations.** Now in `APES_COMBINATIONS` (`apes-play.ts`), from APES2 pp. 37–38. A plate: which combo is this work sitting in? Not a personality quiz. Full 360 is the last row. Not yet a unique interior — data is ready for pass 7.

Mindset routes (`/board/apes/analytical` etc.) must stay registered in `frameworkTopics` or the compass 404s.

**Social engine.** First exclusive landscape engine, on `/board/mapping/cultural`. A.P.E.S. Social looking at culture around this plot. Unlit until mapping cultural room is real (`02-mapping.md`).

## Interconnect

Compass letters on the overview enter mindset rooms. Identity bits are the material. Process uses A.P.E.S. when a stage is a decision (especially 4→5). Generate must interrogate with these letters, not skip to a caption.

## Look

Already more playable than other faculties — do not flatten to sheets. Paper/Ink. Letters as chamfered plates.

## Done when

You can switch a mindset on, see this plot’s mission through that lens, run a bounded cycle, and refuse to ship because one lens has not spoken. Combinations exist as a readable plate sourced from APES2. Cultural/Social has a visible engine socket.

## Do not
