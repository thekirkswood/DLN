# One space — systems inside the board

2026-10-07. Ewan: the unique rooms are about 5%. The walk is still a 2D map of links that dumps you onto a sprawl of text. That is not the board.

## What this is

The board is **one place**. Landscape, identity, the thing itself, the stack, the seats, the pad — they are regions of the same table. Entering a place **zooms that plate**. The interior happens *there*. The rest of the table stays as the world around the work: dimmed, still readable, still the neighbours.

It is not a website of rooms. It is not a menu. It is not an article with twenty doors at the foot.

## What is wrong now

`BoardSpace` is the geography. Click still `router.push`es `/board/mapping/…`, `/board/plot/mission`, `/board/process/stage-1`. Those routes are real interiors. Walking them feels like a second site: chrome, rails, “on this avenue”, “other cells they populate”. Nothing about that is a system inside one board. Pleasure of entering a room is missing.

The unique interiors stay. They move **into** the table. The routes remain as fallbacks and as Open the book, not as the sitting.

## How a sitting works

1. `/board` is the only walking path. Plot in `?plot=`. Cell in `?enter=`.
2. `enter` is a cell key: `plot:mission`, `mapping:advocates`, `mapping:audience:3`, `comms:message`, `process:stage-1`, `workbench:host`, `apes:analytical`, `plot` (centre showcase).
3. Click a plate → that region comes forward → the interior mounts in a well **on the table**. No new document.
4. Neighbours are the other plates still on the table, lighting from the plot file. Not a list of links under a lede.
5. Teaching stays behind (`What this cell holds`). Generate / Assets stay sockets.
6. Leave the room: back to the table (`/board?plot=`). The book (`?book=1`) is still the heavy compile, explicitly named.

## Look

Paper / Ink. Aktiv Grotesk. 45° chamfer. The motion is the craft: orbit yaw and pitch, zoom like a map until a plate becomes the room, the well replaces that plate. Back / zoom-out fully closes the well. Reduced motion: skip orbit, keep enter. Not bounce. Not a game engine unless CSS cannot carry it.

Inside the well: the unique interior’s **body** (the job of that cell). Hide the sprawl chrome — identity rails of twenty names, avenue door lists, “back to faculty”. Those are website IA. Scale of type can be large (mission, message). A quiet room stays quiet (purpose, philosophy).

## Interconnect

The plot file is still the master entity. Holding a line in the well lights the plate you came from, and the centre, when you return. Walking from mission to a seat is clicking the seat on the table, or a pointed seat inside the well that stays on `/board?enter=mapping:audience:n`.

## How agents work this

Do not add more article pages. If a section still lives only as `/board/{avenue}/{topic}`, mount it through `BoardInside` on the table. Strip rails when `board-space.is-inside`. Solport and identity-faculty still to build — they open in the well, not as a new site.

## Done when

A sitting can turn the table, enter landscape, write who speaks, glance at a seat still on the right, enter mission without leaving the desk, see the centre morph, and never feel they opened a CMS. Empty plots still look like a board.
