# The board gives back

2026-10-08. Ewan: ~11%. Structure takes input and displays it. That is the foundation. It does not yet process, research, or return. Paying customers. The more they put in, the more attuned the output. Most of it automated. Ground-up, nothing left behind. Unique approach is the stage walk and A.P.E.S. Do not fake generate. Do not tick landed.

## Two kinds of cell

| Kind | Who writes | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **Hold** | They (and we, in a sitting) | Mission, message, object, seats, cadence… |
| **Return** | The system, from the book | Derived today. Research and generate when a studio key exists |

Not every plate is a form. Some plates are **readouts**. Empty readout is vacant on purpose, not a textarea.

## Three return pipes

1. **Derived** (no model). A cell is a function of other cells. Mission for-whom lights from named seats. Showcase hero is already mission. Spine fill is already held stages. Scale flag changes how empty rooms speak. Competitor / demographic / “why don’t you” plates wait until research lands — they must not invent numbers.
2. **Research** (cheap, our side). Market, competitors, primary / secondary / tertiary seats. Input is what they held plus what we already know. Output writes into mapping seats and landscape tokens as packs, marked as returned, editable. Human sovereign (HR-01). Socket until a keyed job exists.
3. **Generate** (keyed). Copy, stills, social, later talk-to. Prompt is the whole plot book + this cell + Assets stills. Plan `10-generate.md`. Socket until a studio key. Never a dummy chat.

The more they hold, the more attuned 2 and 3 become. Linked: social generate reads message + channel + personality + seats. Other studio products later read the same book.

## Customisation

Not a theme toggle. Customisation is the plot: their mark, colour, type, idea, once agreed, stored on the book. Return pipes dress in that. Paying customer. One brain, Design Lab North’s method (eight-stage + A.P.E.S.), followed for each plot.

## Phone / small board

Same walk as the large table: zoom, enter a well, back closes it. On a small board, drag and wheel **move** the table so every plate can be pulled into view — it must not sit at the bottom showing only Landscape. Orbit is alt/shift. 3D does not switch off at a width. Overflow stays inside the board — never the browser page. Back is an arrow, top left of the well. Text is not selectable on the table while moving; fields inside a well still are.

## Do not

Invent analytics. Fake market research. Fake generate. One textarea per cell forever. Tick landed.
